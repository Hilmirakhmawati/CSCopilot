# Manual Checking vs CSCopilot — Comparison Categories

Use this to structure a before/after evaluation for the client.

## Categories

| # | Category | Components to Compare | Example Metrics |
|---|----------|------------------------|------------------|
| 1 | **Speed** | First response, review, resolution | Average handling time, first-response time, resolution time |
| 2 | **Productivity** | Tickets handled, workload capacity | Tickets per agent/day, tickets per hour, backlog cleared |
| 3 | **Accuracy** | Answer correctness, categorization, sentiment detection | Accuracy rate, error rate, misclassification rate |
| 4 | **Response quality** | Completeness, clarity, tone, personalization | Quality score, rubric score, customer satisfaction |
| 5 | **Consistency** | Standard wording, policy application, agent variance | Score variation between agents, policy adherence rate |
| 6 | **Customer outcomes** | Customer satisfaction and follow-up needs | CSAT, NPS, first-contact resolution, reopen rate |
| 7 | **Escalation quality** | Correct identification of complex cases | Appropriate escalation rate, missed escalation rate |
| 8 | **Agent experience** | Effort, confidence, usability, training | Agent satisfaction, perceived workload, training time |
| 9 | **Cost efficiency** | Labor and operational cost | Cost per ticket, cost per resolution, estimated hours saved |
| 10 | **Compliance and risk** | Privacy, approvals, auditability, policy violations | PII incidents, unauthorized responses, audit completeness |
| 11 | **System reliability** | Availability and technical performance | Uptime, response latency, failed suggestions, integration errors |
| 12 | **Adoption and trust** | Actual usage and agent acceptance | Usage rate, suggestion acceptance rate, override rate, feedback score |
