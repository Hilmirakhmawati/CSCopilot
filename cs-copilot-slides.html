10 · Risks & scope boundary

Make the edges explicit.

Stale source contentMedium

Mitigation: sync status, last-updated label, owner per source.

Hallucinated responseHigh

Mitigation: source-grounded draft + visible reference + human review.

Scope expands too earlyMedium

Mitigation: MVP stays focused on one searchable workflow.

Low agent adoptionMedium

Mitigation: test with real cases, not only synthetic demos.
